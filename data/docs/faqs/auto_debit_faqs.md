# Auto-Debit FAQs

**Q: Is auto-debit available for my policy?**
A: It depends on your policy's current payment terms, which Nexus
checks live rather than assuming from general program descriptions. See
the Premium Payment Policy document for the general program and the
Auto-Debit Policy document for the original 2023 announcement.

**Q: My policy supports auto-debit — will it always go through?**
A: Not necessarily. The linked bank account also has to be in a status
that allows outbound scheduled debits (see the Account Status and
Outbound Payments document). A policy supporting auto-debit and an
account being eligible to fund it are two separate, independently
checked things.

**Q: How do I set up auto-debit?**
A: Ask Nexus, or visit a branch, to link a bank account to your policy.
