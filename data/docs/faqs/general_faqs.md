# General FAQs

**Q: How do I check my account balance?**
A: Ask Nexus for your account summary, or check the Meridian app.

**Q: How do I file an insurance claim?**
A: Ask Nexus to help you start a claim, or use the Meridian app's claims
section. See the Claims Handling Policy document for status definitions.

**Q: Can Nexus make payments on my behalf?**
A: Nexus's `send_customer_notification` tool can send you messages, but
payment scheduling (like auto-debit setup) is recorded on your policy
and account records, not initiated ad-hoc through chat.

**Q: What happens if I mention financial hardship, a safety concern, or
ask for a compliance exception?**
A: These are always reviewed by a human team member, regardless of how
confident Nexus is in handling the rest of your request.
