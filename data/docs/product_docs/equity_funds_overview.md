# Equity Fund Products

Meridian's equity funds (product codes `PROD-EQ-*`, `PROD-GL-*`,
`PROD-FX-*`) invest primarily in listed shares and are generally suited
to customers with an AGGRESSIVE or MODERATE risk profile and a long
investment horizon (5+ years).

## Suitability

`wealth_server.check_product_suitability` evaluates a product against a
submitted risk profile only — it does not use this document directly.
This document exists to give customer-facing context and FAQ grounding,
while the authoritative suitability rule lives in the structured
`investment_products.risk_rating` field.

## Key risks

Equity funds can lose value over short periods. Customers should be
informed that past performance does not guarantee future returns.
