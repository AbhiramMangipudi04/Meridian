# Hybrid and Gold Fund Products

Meridian's hybrid funds (product codes `PROD-HY-*`) blend equity and
debt holdings to target MODERATE risk profiles. Meridian's gold savings
fund (`PROD-GL-01`) tracks domestic gold prices and is often used as a
portfolio diversifier rather than a primary holding.

## Suitability

Suitability is always determined from the structured
`investment_products.risk_rating` field via
`check_product_suitability`, not from this descriptive document.
