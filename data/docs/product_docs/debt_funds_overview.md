# Debt Fund Products

Meridian's debt funds (product codes `PROD-DT-*`) invest in government
and corporate bonds and are generally suited to CONSERVATIVE risk
profiles seeking stable, lower-volatility returns.

## Suitability

As with all products, the authoritative suitability check is
`wealth_server.check_product_suitability(product_id, risk_profile)`
against the structured `risk_rating` field — this document is
explanatory context only.

## Typical Use Case

Customers nearing a financial goal (e.g. funding a near-term purchase)
are often directed toward debt funds instead of equity funds to reduce
volatility risk.
